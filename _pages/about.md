<p>Welcome to my personal webpage!</p>

<p>I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Accounting at the Carlson School of Management, University of Minnesota, and am on the 2026–2027 job market. Prior to Minnesota, I earned an M.S. in Accounting from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and a B.A. in Accounting from Southwestern University of Finance and Economics. I also gained industry experience at Guotai Junan Securities and the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.</p>

<p>My research examines how accounting standards, financial regulation, and individual motivations shape the behavior and information production of capital-market professionals, particularly financial analysts. More broadly, I am interested in how these forces affect firms’ information environments, capital-market outcomes, and real economic decisions.</p>

<p>My teaching interests include financial accounting, accounting data analytics, and managerial accounting. I independently taught Introduction to Financial Reporting at the University of Minnesota and received the Ph.D. Excellence in Teaching Award.</p>

<p>In my spare time, I enjoy playing pickleball and badminton with friends, traveling, trying new restaurants, cooking, and singing.</p>
